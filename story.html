Story

Proud to serve Durham since 1957.

From The Chicken Box on Pine Street to The Chicken Hut on Fayetteville — second-oldest continually operating restaurant in Durham.

Family

Owners Peggy and Claiborne Tapp III

We look forward to seeing you soon — to share a good conversation, fellowship, and a GREAT meal.

1957

The Chicken Hut, originally known as The Chicken Box, was established by the late Claiborne Tapp Jr. on what is now South Roxboro Street (initially Pine Street).

Late 1960s

The entire block was demolished by the city of Durham using urban renewal funds. Chicken Box moved to Fayetteville Street and became The Chicken Hut.

Today

Still family-run — Tre Tapp alongside cousin and aunts — continuing the legacy. Designated City of Durham Landmark. Eater named The Chicken Hut among the best fried chicken spots in America (2022).

Community

Our hearts are full when our city is fed.

We continue to serve delicious home-cooked meals to Durham and the surrounding area — dine-in, take-out, or catered.
